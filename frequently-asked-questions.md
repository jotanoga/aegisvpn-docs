# General

## What is AegisVPN?

AegisVPN is a Virtual Private Network (VPN) service that encrypts your Internet connection and routes your traffic through secure servers to improve privacy, security and online freedom.

---

## How does a VPN work?

When you connect to AegisVPN:

1. Your Internet traffic is encrypted.
2. Your real IP address is hidden.
3. Your traffic is routed through a secure VPN server.
4. Websites see the VPN server's IP address instead of yours.

---

## Why should I use a VPN?

A VPN helps you:

- Protect your privacy.
- Secure your connection on public Wi-Fi.
- Prevent ISP tracking.
- Reduce online profiling.
- Access content while travelling.

# Connections

## How do I connect?

1. Open AegisVPN.
2. Sign in.
3. Choose a server.
4. Click **Connect**.
   
See the [Getting Started Guide](getting-started-guide.md#connect-to-your-first-vpn-server) for a complete walkthrough.

---

## Which server should I choose?

For most users:

- Choose **Fastest Available**, or
- Select a nearby country.

Use servers in specific countries only when necessary.

For additional recommendations, see [Getting Started Guide](getting-started-guide.md#choose-the-best-server).

---

## Why is my IP address different?

This is expected.

AegisVPN replaces your public IP address with the VPN server's IP address.

---

## Can I switch servers?

Yes.

Disconnect from the current server and connect to another location at any time.

---

# Security

## Does AegisVPN encrypt my traffic?

Yes.

All VPN traffic is encrypted using modern VPN protocols and strong encryption standards.

---

## What is Kill Switch?

Kill Switch blocks Internet traffic if the VPN connection unexpectedly disconnects, preventing accidental exposure of your real IP address.

For configuration instructions, see [Configuration Guide](configuration-guide.md#kill-switch).

---

## Should I keep Kill Switch enabled?

Yes.

It is recommended for maximum privacy and security.

---

## What is Split Tunneling?

Split Tunneling lets you choose which applications use the VPN connection while allowing others to access the Internet normally.

See [Configuration Guide](configuration-guide.md#split-tunneling).

---

## What is Secure DNS?

Secure DNS prevents DNS leaks and protects your DNS requests from being monitored by third parties.

---

# Performance

## Will a VPN reduce my Internet speed?

A small reduction in speed is normal because your traffic is encrypted.

For the best performance:

- Use nearby servers.
- Choose WireGuard®.
- Avoid congested servers.

---

## Which protocol is the fastest?

WireGuard® generally provides the best combination of speed, security and stability.

For a comparison of all available protocols, see [Configuration Guide](configuration-guide.md#vpn-protocol).

---

## Why is my connection slow?

Possible reasons include:

- Server congestion.
- Poor Internet connection.
- Long-distance VPN server.
- Local network issues.

Try connecting to another nearby server.

See [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting-guide.md#slow-internet-speed).

---

# Troubleshooting

## I can't connect.

Try the following:

- Restart the application.
- Restart your device.
- Switch servers.
- Change VPN protocol.
- Verify your Internet connection.
  
 See the complete [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting-guide.md#unable-to-connect-to-a-vpn-server).

---

## The application won't open.

Restart your device.

If the issue continues:

- Reinstall AegisVPN.
- Update your operating system.
  
See [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting-guide.md#application-wont-start).

---

## Kill Switch blocks my Internet.

Reconnect to the VPN.

If necessary, temporarily disable Kill Switch from:

**Settings → Privacy & Security**

See [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting-guide.md#kill-switch-blocks-internet-after-disconnecting).

---

## I cannot sign in.

Verify:

- Your email address.
- Your password.
- Your Internet connection.

Reset your password if necessary.

See [Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting-guide.md#incorrect-username-or-password).

# Privacy

## Does AegisVPN log my activity?

No.

AegisVPN follows a strict no-logs policy and does not store browsing activity or connection history.

---

## Can my ISP see what I'm doing?

Your ISP can detect that you're connected to a VPN but cannot see your encrypted browsing activity.

---

## Does AegisVPN protect me on public Wi-Fi?

Yes.

It encrypts your traffic, reducing the risk of data interception on unsecured networks.
