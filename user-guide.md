## Overview

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) encrypts your Internet traffic and routes it through a secure remote server. This protects your online activity, hides your IP address, and helps prevent unauthorized access to your data.

This guide explains how to install, configure, and use the VPN application for everyday browsing and remote work.

---

## Audience

This guide is intended for:

- Individual users
- Remote workers
- Business users
- Travelers using public Wi-Fi networks

*No previous technical knowledge is required.*

## System Requirements

Before installing the application, make sure your device meets the following requirements:

- Windows 10 or later
- 200 MB available disk space

- macOS 12 or later

- Android 10+

4. Grant the required permissions.

## Signing In

1. Open the application.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign In**.

If you do not have an account, select **Create Account**.

---

## Connecting

To establish a secure connection:

1. Open the application.
2. Select a server location.
3. Click **Connect**.
4. Wait until the status changes to **Connected**.

Your Internet traffic is now encrypted and secured.

---

## Features

### Auto Connect

Automatically connects the VPN when your device starts.

Recommended for:

- Remote workers
- Frequent travelers

---

### Kill Switch

Blocks Internet traffic if the VPN connection is interrupted.

This prevents accidental exposure of your real IP address, DNS or WebRTC, as well as your personal data.

---

### Split Tunneling

Allows selected applications to bypass the VPN connection.

Use this feature when only specific applications require encryption.

---

### Dedicated IP

Provides a unique IP address exclusively for your account.

Recommended for:

- Remote access
- Business applications
- Banking services

---

## Best Practices

For maximum privacy:

- Always connect before browsing.
- Enable Kill Switch.
- Keep the application updated.
- Avoid unknown public Wi-Fi whenever possible.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Unable to connect

Possible causes:

- No Internet connection
- Firewall restrictions
- Incorrect credentials

Try the following:

1. Restart the application.
2. Restart your device.
3. Try another server.
4. Check your Internet connection.

---

### Slow connection

Possible causes:

- Distant server location
- High server load

Solutions:

- Choose a nearby server.
- Disconnect and reconnect.
- Switch protocol if available.

## FAQ

### Does a VPN make me anonymous?

No.

A VPN significantly improves your privacy but does not provide complete anonymity.

---

### Does a VPN slow down my connection?

A small speed reduction is normal because traffic is encrypted.

Choosing a nearby server usually minimizes the impact.

---

### Can I use the VPN on multiple devices?

Yes.

The maximum number of simultaneous connections depends on your subscription plan.
